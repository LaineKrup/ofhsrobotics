The Osceola Robotics Club is a student-led team focused on learning engineering, programming, and teamwork through competitive robotics and community outreach.

This club is open to all students, especially beginners interested in engineering. We meet regularly to design, build, and program functional robots primarily for the regional FIRST Tech Challenge. Beyond competition, members can explore hands-on engineering and fabrication using our lab’s 3D printer, laser cutter, and other tools to bring their own ideas to life.
